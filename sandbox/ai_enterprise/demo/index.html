Portfolio framing

How the system works

From messy signals to accountable action.

  1. Collect sourcesPaste notes, attach lightweight files, or add source URLs.
  2. Normalize signalsConvert scattered inputs into reviewable rows with source context.
  3. Prioritize and routeScore risk, assign likely owners, and surface business impact.
  4. Cluster patternsExpose repeated issues and likely duplicate groups before handoff.
  5. Human reviewEdit the AI output, save the session, and export structured next steps.

Enterprise AI should explain itself.

Human-in-the-loop, not autopilotAI drafts the triage; people own the decision.
Explain before asking for trustEvery recommendation includes reasoning, evidence, confidence, and open questions.
Design for review, not chatThe workflow emphasizes tables, filters, assignment, exports, and accountability.
Make patterns visibleClusters reveal where separate inputs are really the same operational problem.
