AI Sandbox

Two functional AI-assisted products showing how I move from product definition and information architecture through interaction design, visual direction, testing, and front-end implementation.

Kaiju Run | 怪獣ラン

Kaijū Run is a playable AI-assisted browser game that demonstrates end-to-end product design, game UX, visual direction, and front-end implementation.

The experience combines atomic attacks, shield mechanics, enemy waves, gameplay statistics, dynamic content, and trivia into a compact, responsive arcade system.

AI accelerated concept exploration, visual direction, gameplay systems, and front-end development; iterative play-testing and design judgment shaped the final experience.

Case Study Play Demo

AI Enterprise UX Intelligence

AI Enterprise UX Intelligence is a human-in-the-loop enterprise AI triage product that transforms notes and support tickets, URLs and uploaded documents into prioritized issues, ownership recommendations, business impact and confidence scores, and actionable next steps.

The human-in-the-loop workflow helps Product, Design, Research, Support, and Engineering teams move from fragmented evidence to structured action while preserving human review and decision control.
