What I Help With

Bringing clarity to complex product challenges.

I typically support products that have outgrown simple UX patterns: enterprise SaaS, internal tools, dashboards, AI-assisted workflows, data-heavy interfaces, and systems with many states, edge cases, and user roles.

The challenge is rarely just designing a screen. It is creating a product foundation that helps teams move from complexity to clarity with better speed, usability, and alignment.

Where Teams Get Stuck

Complexity creates friction when products scale.

  • Product flows become harder to navigate as features, states, and edge cases grow
  • Inconsistent patterns across screens and teams reduce clarity and trust
  • Design systems often lag behind product needs or never fully take hold
  • AI and data-rich features add complexity that needs better UX framing

The issue usually is not a lack of capability. It is a lack of clarity, cohesion, and scalable decision-making across the product.

How I Work

Understanding the product, the team, and the constraints before prescribing solutions.

I start by looking at the product as it actually exists in practice: the workflows, the interaction model, the system behavior, and the places where teams and users are losing time or clarity.

  • Review current flows, screens, and product structure
  • Identify friction points across workflows, hierarchy, and states
  • Align with product and engineering on constraints, priorities, and opportunities
  • Define the most useful path forward for users and the business

The goal is not to add more process. It is to create the right level of clarity so the team can move faster with more confidence.
