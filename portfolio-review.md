# Portfolio content and positioning review

## Recommended positioning

Use **Product Design** as the primary professional label. Use **UX** as a supporting capability and search term.

The clearest positioning is:

> Senior Product Designer focused on enterprise, AI, and data-intensive platforms.

This matches the resume, supports Senior/Staff/Principal/Founding Product Design searches, and still preserves strong UX credibility through research, workflow design, interaction design, usability testing, and the retained UX Process section.

## Story hierarchy

1. **Apple Topline** — strongest lead case study for enterprise AI, data, product strategy, and systems thinking.
2. **Audi ADAS HMI** — demonstrates safety-critical interaction design, research, and system-level behavior.
3. **Tesla Fleet & Energy** — demonstrates operational platforms, scale, real-time data, and design systems.
4. **Apple Maps** — strong internal-tools case study with a measurable workflow improvement.
5. **eBay HUB** — useful supporting story for behavior design and large-enterprise employee experience, but not a lead case study.
6. **Kaijū Run** — keep in the AI Sandbox as evidence of prototyping and implementation range, not as a primary product-design case study.
